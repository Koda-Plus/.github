## ⚡ What we build

We start every prospect with one question: what does the business actually need? That answer decides the stack, not the other way round.

| | Track | What it means | Stack |
|:--:|---|---|---|
| 🌐 | **Websites** | Brand, marketing and lead-gen sites. No cart, no checkout, no bloat. | Astro |
| 🛒 | **E-commerce** | Real carts, real checkout, B2C and simple B2B. | Astro + Medusa.js |
| 🏭 | **B2B wholesale portals** | Customer-group pricing, VAT-ID registration, MOQ, quick-order, credit limits, ERP sync. | Astro + Medusa.js + B2B modules |
| 🧩 | **Web apps** | Dashboards, CRMs, internal tools, AI agents and integrations. | Astro / Next.js / Node |

We also cover the work around the system: graphic design, process automation, and SEO/AEO.

## 🎯 How we work: proof, not a pitch

- **The demo comes first.** Before the first call we build a working demo of your store, with your products and your brand. You click it, you judge it.
- **We do not lock you in.** Open source at the core (Medusa.js, Astro, Node). All the code and every access belongs to you, from day one.
- **We ship in weeks, not quarters.** A fleet of AI agents works in parallel on top of our own knowledge base of standards, playbooks and tested modules, with us reviewing the architecture and quality.

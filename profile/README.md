## ⚡ What we build

Every project starts with one question: what does the business actually need? The answer picks the stack, not the other way round.

| | Track | What it means | Stack |
|:--:|---|---|---|
| 🏭 | **B2B wholesale portals** | Customer-group pricing, VAT-ID registration, MOQ and packaging, quick-order, credit limits and payment terms, price negotiations, ERP sync. | Medusa.js + B2B modules |
| 🛒 | **E-commerce** | Real carts and real checkout, B2C and simple B2B. Migrations off Shopify and Webflow included. | Medusa.js + Astro or Next.js |
| 🌐 | **Websites** | Brand, marketing and lead-gen sites, with an editable CMS where the team needs one. | Astro, Next.js + Payload |
| 🧩 | **Web apps and AI agents** | CRMs, dashboards, internal tools, customer service agents and process automation. | Next.js, Node, Claude |

Around the system we also do graphic design, SEO and AEO, and motion (product films rendered in Blender).

## 🎯 How we work: proof, not a pitch

- **The demo comes first.** Before the first call we build a working demo for your industry. It is raw on purpose: our branding, test data, the engine running for real. You click it, you judge it.
- **We do not lock you in.** Open source at the core (Medusa.js, Astro, Next.js, Node). All the code and every access belongs to you, from day one.
- **We ship in weeks, not quarters.** Several AI agents work in parallel on top of our own knowledge base of standards, playbooks and tested modules. We review the architecture and everything that reaches the client.
- **Close to the source.** We work with the team behind Medusa.js and are listed in the Storesynk experts directory.

## 📦 Open source: Medusa v2 plugins

Fifteen plugins for Medusa v2, built for the Polish and EU market and running in our client stores. Code in [**medusa-integrations**](https://github.com/Koda-Plus/medusa-integrations), packages on npm under `@koda-plus`.

| Area | Plugins |
|---|---|
| **Marketplaces and ERP** | [Allegro](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-allegro), [BaseLinker](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-baselinker), [OLX](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-olx), [Subiekt nexo PRO](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-subiekt-nexo) |
| **Payments, invoices, shipping** | [Stripe (BLIK, Przelewy24)](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-stripe), [Fakturownia + KSeF](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-fakturownia), [InPost](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-inpost) |
| **B2B** | [Packaging and MOQ](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-packaging), [Trade credit](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-trade-credit), [Negotiations](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-negotiations), [VAT whitelist + VIES](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-vat-whitelist) |
| **Compliance and retention** | [EU compliance (GPSR, GDPR, Omnibus)](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-compliance), [Loyalty](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-loyalty), [E-mails](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-emails) |
| **Team** | [Tasks](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@koda-plus/medusa-plugin-tasks): a task board inside the Medusa admin |

Every plugin ships with a demo mode. Writes to outside systems are off by default and run only from a plan you read first. More at [koda.plus/en/integrations](https://www.koda.plus/en/integrations).
